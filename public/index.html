About us

Professional groomers, gentle hands.

At Grooming with MK, we believe grooming shouldn't be a chore for you or a stressful ordeal for your pet. We bring a cage-free spa experience directly to your doorstep, so there's no carrier and no car ride — just a calmer, happier pet. Our groomers treat every cat and dog with the patience and care they deserve, pairing years of hands-on experience with a gentle touch to keep them healthy, comfortable and looking their best.

We're a Nairobi-based mobile grooming team offering everything from a simple bath and brush to a full groom: bathing, medicated baths, blow-dry and coat trim, dematting, nail trims, ear cleaning, teeth cleaning and sanitary trimming. We're animal people first — this is a service we love doing, in the comfort of your own home.

The growing demand for dog disinfection baths in Nairobi reflects a rising awareness of proper pet hygiene. These baths keep dogs clean and protected from harmful germs while supporting a safer home for the whole family. As responsible pet ownership grows, professional sanitisation has become an essential part of keeping pets healthy.

Regular brushing does more than improve appearance — it prevents matting, promotes a healthy coat and keeps your pet comfortable. It's also one of the easiest ways to bond with your pet, and our groomers make it a calm, unhurried part of every visit.

Medicated baths help manage skin conditions, reduce parasites, treat infections and ease allergy-related discomfort. Using suitable products and professional technique, we help improve skin and coat condition for a happier, more comfortable dog.

Good oral hygiene is about more than fresh breath. Our teeth cleaning gives personalised attention to keep teeth clean and healthy, improving comfort and overall wellness for the long run.

Removing tangled, matted fur is about comfort as much as appearance — mats can pull on skin and trap moisture. Our dematting is done patiently and gently, an important part of responsible ongoing coat care.

Sanitary trims keep the areas prone to soiling clean and comfortable. It's a small service with an outsized effect on hygiene, and one we include as standard in most grooming packages.

A proper blow-dry and trim is about more than a stylish finish — it supports coat health and comfort too. With the right tools and technique, we leave coats clean, even and well-kept.

Overgrown nails can cause real discomfort and even affect how your pet walks. Regular, careful trims keep paws healthy and your pet moving with ease and confidence.

Gentle, thorough ear cleaning reduces the risk of infection and helps us spot potential issues early. It's a quick step that plays a real role in your pet's long-term comfort.

Your pet can stay at our facilities, play and socialise with other animals, while you take care of what you need to. A relaxed, supervised break for them — and one less thing for you to worry about.
